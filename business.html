FAQ

How can we help?

Can I rent out my own car on the platform?

Yes, you can list your car on our platform and earn money by renting it out to verified drivers. We provide comprehensive insurance coverage and handle all the administrative aspects.

How does car sharing work?

Car sharing allows you to rent a car directly through the app in just a few steps. Choose a nearby car, book it for the time you need, unlock it with your phone, and start driving. When you're done, simply park the car and end the trip in the app.

Is my car insured during rentals?

Yes, all cars on our platform are covered by comprehensive insurance during rental periods. We offer three insurance packages to choose from, ensuring your car is protected.

How do payments and earnings work?

Payments are processed automatically through the app. Car owners receive earnings directly to their account, and renters are charged only for the time they use the car. All transactions are transparent and secure.

What happens if the car gets damaged?

In case of damage, we have a clear process in place. Both parties document the car's condition before and after the trip. Our insurance coverage handles repairs, and we ensure fair resolution for all parties involved.

Who can rent a car on the app?

Anyone with a valid driver's license and a verified account can rent cars through our app. We verify all users to ensure safety and security for car owners and renters alike.
