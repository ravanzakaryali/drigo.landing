No security deposit. Clear rental details.

DRIGO does not require a security deposit. You still pay the rental charges and any applicable fees. Before confirming, check these details for your chosen car:

  • Fuel: included on eligible trips. Follow the partner-station refuelling instructions shown in the app.
  • Parking: included in designated zones. Check the zone rules; other parking can be chargeable.
  • Insurance: basic insurance is included, with optional protection tiers. Excesses, conditions and exclusions apply; see insurance details.
  • Mileage: check the kilometres included in your package and the charge for extra distance.
  • Other charges: review tolls, fines, reservation fees and any selected extras in the app and payment terms.

How to rent a car with DRIGO

  1. Create your account. Download the app and complete identity and driving-licence verification. You must meet the rental eligibility requirements.
  2. Find a car. Use the app map to check actual availability near you in Dubai. Select the car and compare its packages.
  3. Review and book. Check the price, included services, insurance and return conditions, then confirm your booking.
  4. Check and unlock. Follow the app's vehicle-condition photo checklist, report any existing damage and unlock the car with your phone.
  5. Return and finish. Park in an allowed return zone, complete the app checklist and confirm that the rental has ended.

Use the DRIGO app for current vehicle locations and service areas. The illustrated Dubai map on the website is not a live availability map, and does not promise that a car is available in every neighbourhood.

You can drive within the UAE during your rental. To finish a trip that started in Dubai, return to a designated zone in Dubai and end the rental in the app. A rental that starts in Sharjah must finish in a designated zone in Sharjah. Check the permitted zones before you set off.

For assistance, use the in-app Support Chat. DRIGO is an app-based service and does not provide walk-in customer reception.

Dubai car rental questions

Can I rent without a security deposit?

Yes. DRIGO offers car rental without a security deposit. Rental charges, applicable fees and any damage liability remain subject to your booking and the Terms.

Can visitors rent a car?

Eligibility depends on your documents and the rental requirements. Complete verification in the app before planning a booking, and read Section 2 of the Terms for the full requirements. A valid licence alone does not guarantee approval.

Is fuel included in a monthly rental?

Fuel is included on eligible trips. Check the specific monthly package in the app; do not assume the same inclusions apply to every duration or vehicle.

Can I return a Dubai rental in Sharjah?

To end the rental, return to a designated zone in the city where the trip started. A Dubai rental must end in Dubai, even if you drive to Sharjah during the rental.

Is DRIGO car sharing or car rental?

DRIGO offers app-based car sharing in Dubai and Sharjah with daily and monthly rental packages. You find, book and unlock the car in the app, and return it according to the service-zone rules.
