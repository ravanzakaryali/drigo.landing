    Questions, answered.

    Can’t find what you need? Write to us and the Drigo team will get back to you.

    Contact support ?
    01How does the app work?

    Open Drigo, pick a car on the map, choose a daily or monthly rental, and unlock it with your phone when you reach it.

    02Where can I find a car?

    Open the Drigo app for actual availability and parking zones in Dubai and Sharjah. You can drive and park across both cities during your rental. To end it, return to a designated zone in the city where you started: Dubai rentals end in Dubai, and Sharjah rentals end in Sharjah. The city on this website is an illustration.

    03Is a security deposit required?

    Drigo offers rentals without a security deposit. Review your rental price, fees, and payment details in the app before booking.

    04What’s included in my rental?

    Fuel is included on eligible trips, with free parking in designated zones. Review the insurance options, coverage conditions, and full rental details in the app.

    05What do I need to get started?

    Create an account and complete verification. You’ll need a valid driving licence and must meet the rental eligibility requirements.

    06Is the car insured during my rental?

    Every rental comes with insurance cover. The app shows the cover options for your trip and what each one includes before you book. The full conditions are in Section 8 of the Terms.

    07What happens if the car gets damaged?

    Report it in the app straight away and follow the steps shown. What you pay depends on your cover and the circumstances, as set out in Section 9 of the Terms.

    08Can I list my own car on Drigo?

    Yes. Rental companies and individual car owners can tell us about their fleet through the For business form, and the Drigo team will get in touch.
